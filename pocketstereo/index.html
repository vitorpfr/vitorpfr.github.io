Music, and nothing else

The whole point is what it doesn't do.

◎

A record you actually spin

Drag around the platter to move through your library, with a haptic tick for every step. Tap the label at the centre to choose. It's a deliberate, physical way to pick music — not another scrolling list.

◐

Focus mode

Full screen, no system clutter, its own battery readout. Put the phone down and let the album play. Focus is free, and always will be.

♫

Your music, wherever it lives

Apple Music and the songs already on your iPhone, together or filtered to one source. Pocket Stereo tells you exactly what it found from each, so nothing feels missing.

◈

Four ways to look

Turntable and Minimal are free; Mono and Retro come with Pro. Each one restyles the whole player — palette, type, and the feel of the controls — not just its colour.

Pricing

No subscriptions, ever.

Free to start — no account and no trial: full playback of your whole library, the Turntable and Minimal themes, and focus mode. Playing music is never behind a paywall.
