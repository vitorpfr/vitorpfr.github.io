Common questions

  • Spindle isn't showing my music. Spindle needs Media & Apple Music access to read your library. Check Settings › Spindle on your iPhone, or Settings › Privacy & Security › Media & Apple Music. If access is on and the library still looks short, open Settings › Library inside Spindle — it may be filtered to just Apple Music or just Local Files, and it reports how many songs it found from each.
  • Some Apple Music songs are missing. Tracks that can't actually be played are hidden rather than shown as dead ends. That usually means catalog content without an active Apple Music subscription. When a whole list is empty for that reason, Spindle says so instead of showing you a blank screen. Music you've downloaded or synced to the device still plays.
  • Do I need an Apple Music subscription? No. Spindle plays the music already on your iPhone — downloaded, synced, or purchased. A subscription only adds streaming from the Apple Music catalog. Playback and focus mode are never behind Spindle's own paid unlock either.
  • iOS asked to change my app icon. That's expected. Each theme carries its own home-screen icon, so choosing a theme changes the icon to match — and iOS always shows a confirmation when any app changes its icon. It only happens when you pick a theme, never on its own and never when you simply open the app.
  • I bought Spindle Pro on another device — how do I restore it? Open Settings › Unlock Spindle Pro and choose Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
  • The wheel isn't scrolling. The platter scrolls by dragging around it, like spinning a record — a tap on the platter won't move the selection. Tap the label at the centre to choose the highlighted item.
